# Automated PDF stress fixtures

`stress-a4-portrait-100pages.pdf` is a generated, copyright-free synthetic
fixture used for automated PDF analysis, workspace, PDFium thumbnail, and
cancellation stress tests.

Classification: `AUTOMATED_STRESS_FIXTURE`

The fixture contains 100 A4 portrait pages at rotation 0, with a page label,
simple corner markers, a thin line, and a small per-page unique marker. It is
not a Hancom visual-compatibility golden and is not evidence of 100-section
HWPX compatibility.
